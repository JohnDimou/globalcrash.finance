7 of the 9 bear-market episodes since 1990 had this index at 50 or higher six months before peak stress — computed from the same series on the chart, re-graded at every print.

Base rates, against ourselves. 53% of ALL months since 1990 read 50 or higher — so "above 50" alone is weak evidence, and the table above should be read with that in mind. Of the 112 months at 60+, 46% were false alarms — no anchored episode within the following 12 months. That is the noise-to-signal trade-off (Kaminsky & Reinhart) every early-warning system pays; we publish ours instead of hiding it. Monthly granularity.

The honest miss: COVID-19. The index read low into January 2020 — a pandemic is invisible to financial data until it hits. What the gauge measures is how much fragility a shock lands on, not the shock itself.
